Three wrappers, three gaps

stocks (HSI)  <--tracking-->  fund insides (NAV)
        ^                         <--premium-->  2800 share
        +----- quoted basis -----  HSIF

Premium asks if the share is rich or cheap versus official NAV. Tracking asks if the fund's stocks follow the cash index (adj NAV, cash stripped). Quoted basis asks if the future is rich or cheap versus a fair future; that still needs dividend yield q, so fair stays NA.

Hedge 2800 with HSIF and you still own all three. Hedge is not flatten. Create band is stamp 0.1% plus 2 bp/side commission assumed; borrow is NA. Implied (r-q) is not implied r until q exists. 2800-vs-HSIF residual is 1:1 notional, beta assumed.
